# Dominic IGYE

**ICT operations · Cybersecurity · ICT governance · AI for compliance**

I work where IT operations, security and accountability meet: running reliable networks and systems, securing them, and making them ready for audit. I'm completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence, researching how agentic AI can be governed safely in regulatory and compliance work.

### 📂 Projects
- **[ICT Risk Register & KPI Dashboard](https://github.com/dtigye/ict-risk-register-and-kpi-dashboard)**: an Excel risk register with automatic scoring and governance checks (missing owners, overdue reviews and treatments), plus Python KPI reporting on service availability, incidents, service-request SLAs and patch compliance, mapped to ISO/IEC 27001, COBIT and ITIL.
- **[Audit Analytics in SQL](https://github.com/dtigye/audit-analytics-sql)** *(in progress, Phase 1 of 5)*: SQL tests an auditor runs over finance and access records, from data validation to duplicate payments, split purchases, Benford's law and segregation of duties. Runs on SQLite and PostgreSQL, with every test checked against a known answer key.

*Both projects use synthetic data for a fictional organisation.*

### 🔬 Current work
**Runtime-governed screening**: a research prototype for agentic sanctions and PEP screening. It adds a runtime control plane that enforces tool allow-listing, role checks, segregation of duties and human approval, and keeps a hash-chained audit trail. It's evaluated on a synthetic, adversarial case catalogue (prompt injection, tool poisoning, stale lists). *Code to be published after MSc examination.*
